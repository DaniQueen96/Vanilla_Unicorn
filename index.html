GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How do I book an event?

Contact Vanilla Unicorn management in city to discuss your plans. We'll prepare a personalised quote and confirm the arrangements with you.

Can I book a private area?

Yes. Ask about our VIP room, front booth or exclusive venue hire. Access and availability are agreed individually.

Can I organise a surprise celebration?

Absolutely. Tell us what you're planning and we'll discuss the details discreetly.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. Services, entertainment, drinks and optional extras can be combined to suit your event, subject to availability.

Are deposits required?

Smaller services are generally paid for when provided. Larger events may require a flexible deposit agreed during your booking consultation.

What happens if my plans change?

Contact VU management. Changes, rescheduling and customer cancellations are reviewed individually, including any expenses already committed.

Is there a dress code?

Not generally. Special themed events may have advertised dress requirements, so check the event details.
